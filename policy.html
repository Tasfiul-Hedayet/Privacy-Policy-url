Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Todo-app mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All data you enter into the App (such as tasks and to-do items) is stored only on your device.

2. Local Storage Only

Your data never leaves your device. The App does not use cloud storage, servers, or any external databases. We have no access to your data at any time.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services that collect personal data.

4. Permissions

The App only requests permissions necessary for its core functionality. No data gathered through permissions is transmitted off your device.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing items within the App or by uninstalling the App. Once uninstalled, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as no data is collected at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via GitHub: github.com/tasfiul-hedayet
