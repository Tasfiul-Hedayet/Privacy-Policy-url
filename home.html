🌱 PlantSage

Privacy Policy & Data Usage
📅 Last updated: September 5, 2026

PlantSage (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a houseplant companion app that helps you discover plants suited to your space and sends smart watering reminders. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal data to make PlantSage work for you:

  • User-provided info: plant names, custom watering schedules, room names (e.g., “living room”), and photos you choose to upload for plant identification.
  • Device data: anonymous usage statistics (e.g., which features you open) to improve the app.
  • Location (optional): if you enable it, we use your general area to suggest plants suitable for your climate. You can disable this in your device settings.
  • Camera access: only if you use the plant identification or leaf-scanning feature. Images are processed locally or sent securely to our AI provider; they are not stored unless you save them to your personal plant library.
🔒 minimal data   We do not collect your name, email, or contact info unless you voluntarily provide it via support requests.

2. How we use your data

  • Plant recommendations: light, temperature, and humidity data (if provided) help us suggest plants that fit your space.
  • Smart reminders: watering, fertilizing, and repotting alerts are based on the schedules you set.
  • App improvement: aggregated analytics help us fix bugs and design better features.
  • Plant identification: images are processed to identify species and provide care advice; they are not used for any other purpose.

3. Data storage & security

Your plant data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable cloud backup (optional), your data is encrypted and stored on secure servers hosted in the EU. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for data in transit.

We regularly review our security practices, but no system is 100% secure. If you have concerns, you can always keep all data offline.

4. Third-party services

PlantSage integrates with a few trusted third-party providers to power plant identification and analytics:

  • AI Plant Identifier API – processes leaf images to return species matches and care tips. Images are deleted immediately after processing.
  • Firebase / Analytics – anonymous usage data to understand feature adoption. No personal identifiers are shared.
  • Push notification service – to deliver watering reminders (only if you opt in). We do not share any of your plant data with this service.

These providers are contractually bound to process data only for the purposes stated and in compliance with GDPR/CCPA.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access – see what data we hold about you (we store very little).
  • Rectify – correct any inaccuracies in your plant data.
  • Delete – delete your account and all associated data at any time via the app settings.
  • Withdraw consent – turn off location, camera, or notifications in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@plantsage.app. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children’s privacy

PlantSage is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Cookies & tracking

PlantSage does not use cookies. We may use anonymous device identifiers to track app usage for analytics; these are reset when you reinstall the app. You can opt out of analytics tracking in the app’s settings under “Privacy”.

8. International data transfers

If you are using PlantSage from outside the EU, your data may be transferred to and processed in servers located in the EU or the US. We ensure appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place with our service providers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via an in-app notice or email (if you have provided one). The latest version will always be available at plantsage.app/privacy.
